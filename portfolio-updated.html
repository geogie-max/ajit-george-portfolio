A bit about me

I grew up in Mumbai, did my undergrad in Electronics and Telecommunication, and moved to Stuttgart in 2023 for the Master's. The INFOTECH programme has been good, with a lot of space to go deep into whatever you're curious about, and I've ended up spending most of my time in haptics, motor control, and robotics.

My thesis, started in April 2026, is on hand retargeting for teleoperation using the Apple Vision Pro and IsaacLab. The goal is to map human hand motion naturally onto a robot hand, accounting for the fact that human and robot hands don't have the same proportions or degrees of freedom. Supervised by Michel Pabst at VISUS.

Since February 2025 I've been working at BelWü, the network that connects every university in Baden-Württemberg. I started with a research project in August 2025: building a threat detection pipeline in Go on top of Apache Kafka, and have since moved into deploying P4-programmable switches and maintaining routing security infrastructure.

Before that, I spent a year at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems writing Python and C++ tools for research on leadership emergence in multi-agent systems. Good introduction to what research-quality code actually looks like.

Outside of work I build things. I built a 3D printer from scratch: frame, electronics, firmware, everything. I design PCBs in KiCad when something I need doesn't exist. I currently have a reaction wheel balancing system on the bench that's taking longer than it should.

Things I've built

Active · Thesis · April 2026–present

Hand Retargeting for VR Teleoperation

The challenge with teleoperating a robot hand is that yours doesn't look the same as the robot's. Different finger lengths, different joint ranges, sometimes different numbers of fingers. This thesis works on retargeting algorithms that translate natural human hand poses into valid robot configurations, tracked via Apple Vision Pro and validated in IsaacLab.

Apple Vision ProIsaacLabROS 2visionOSC++ / PythonMoveIt 2

Supervisor: Michel Pabst, VISUS, University of Stuttgart

Status: Just started. Thesis runs through late 2026.

Context: Part of ongoing VR teleoperation research at VISUS

Active · Personal build · 2026

Reaction Wheel Balancing System

A self-balancing triangle controlled by a reaction wheel. STM32 runs the PID loop, ESP32 handles BLE telemetry, BLDC motor drives the wheel with a current control loop. PCB in KiCad, enclosure in Fusion 360. Still on the bench.

STM32ESP32BLDC / FOCPIDKiCadBLE
Active · Personal pitch · 2026

Haptic Feedback Glove (tPNS)

Exploring transcutaneous peripheral nerve stimulation for VR haptic feedback without bulky gloves. Signal generation on ESP32, electrode placement optimised via COMSOL FEM simulation, custom electrode array. Pitched to Prof. Schmalstieg at VISUS.

ESP32COMSOL FEMtPNSElectrode Design
Completed · ROS 2 · 2026

MoveIt 2 Pick-and-Place with Obstacle Avoidance

6-DOF Panda arm in Gazebo with collision-free trajectory planning via MoveIt 2. OpenCV handles grasp point detection, wrapped in a ROS 2 action server for sequential pick-and-place tasks.

ROS 2MoveIt 2GazeboOpenCVPython
